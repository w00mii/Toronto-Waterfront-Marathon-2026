🦶 Daily foot routine
Every morning before first step

  • Plantar fascia stretch — 30 sec each foot
  • Frozen water bottle roll — 1 min each foot
Fuel & nutrition
⚠️ Your iron is low — address this first
  • Ferritin test — athletes need 50–80+ ng/mL
  • Ferrous bisglycinate — take with Vitamin C, not coffee/tea
  • Iron foods: red meat, lentils, tofu, dark leafy greens
  • Recheck in 8–12 weeks
🍬 When to take gels
  • Under 60 min: water only
  • Over 75 min (16 km+): carb fuel every 30–45 min after first 45 min
  • Over 90 min: 30–60g carbs/hour — practice every long run
🧃 Fuel options
Train your gut — GI issues cause more DNFs than fitness
  • Maurten gels — easiest on stomach, take with water
  • Medjool dates — ~18g carbs, very easy to digest. Start here.
  • Rice balls — rice + salt + honey, highly digestible
  • Nuun tabs — add to water on runs over 60 min
Stay healthy
🦶 Plantar fasciitis
Flares when mileage jumps fast or shoes wear out
  • Morning routine: calf stretch + toe pull + frozen bottle roll, 30 sec/1 min each foot
  • Eccentric calf raises: 3 sec lowering, 3×20, three times/week
  • Replace shoes after 600–700 km
  • If it flares: cut mileage 30–50%, no running through sharp heel pain
🦵 Shin splints
  • 10% rule: never increase weekly km by more than 10%
  • Lateral band walks: weak hips force the lower leg to compensate
  • Toe taps: 3×30 sec standing
  • Sharp shin pain: stop and rest 3–5 days
✅ General habits
  • Warm up: 5 min walk + leg swings, hip circles, high knees
  • Cool down: 5 min walk + static stretches 30–45 sec each
  • Sleep: 7–9 hrs
  • Easy means easy: full conversation pace
